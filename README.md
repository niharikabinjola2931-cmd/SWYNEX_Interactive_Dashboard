# SWYNEX Interactive Healthcare Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project was developed as part of my Data Analyst Internship at SWYNEX Technologies.

The objective was to build an interactive healthcare analytics dashboard to analyze patient admissions, 30-day readmissions, clinical risk factors, healthcare costs, and hospital performance.

## Dashboard Pages

The Power BI dashboard contains five interactive pages:

1. **Executive Overview** — Overall admissions, readmission rates, length of stay, and admission costs.
2. **Readmission Analysis** — Factors associated with 30-day readmissions, including length of stay, ward type, and clinical severity.
3. **Patient & Clinical Analysis** — Patient demographics, insurance, diagnosis categories, and clinical indicators.
4. **Billing & Cost Analysis** — Admission costs, length of stay, readmission rates, and out-of-pocket expenses.
5. **Hospital Analysis** — Hospital capacity, bed availability, and readmission patterns.

## Key Insights

* Overall 30-day readmission rate was **11.84%**.
* Patients staying **15+ days** had the highest 30-day readmission rate at **28.80%**.
* **ICU admissions** had the highest readmission rate among ward types at **23.76%**.
* Readmission rates increased with higher **Charlson Comorbidity Index** values.
* Hospital bed capacity showed very little relationship with 30-day readmission rates.
